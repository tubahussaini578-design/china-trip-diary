My Trip to China

A travel diary by Tubaa Hussaini

Beijing | Other Cities | Travel Tips

Day 1-3: Beijing

My trip began in Beijing, the capital of China. The city mixes ancient history with modern life, and there was something new to see every hour.

Walking the Great Wall

The highlight of my trip was visiting the Great Wall of China. It stretches across mountains for thousands of kilometers, and walking on it felt like stepping back in time. I climbed one section early in the morning, when the air was cool and there were fewer visitors.

  • Visit the Forbidden City, the old palace of the emperors
  • Walk through Tiananmen Square
  • Climb the Great Wall at Badaling or Mutianyu
  • Try Peking duck for dinner

Day 4-7: Other Cities

After Beijing, I traveled to three more famous cities by fast train.

  1. Xi'an: I saw the Terracotta Army, thousands of clay soldiers buried to guard an ancient emperor.
  2. Chengdu: I visited giant pandas at a panda research base and ate spicy Sichuan hotpot.
  3. Shanghai: I walked along the Bund and enjoyed the city skyline lit up at night.

Travel Tips

These tips helped me enjoy the trip:

  • Check your passport and visa requirements well before you travel.
  • Download a translation app and an offline map.
  • Carry some cash, but also set up a mobile payment option.
  • Use the high-speed trains to travel between cities.

Did You Know?

China's high-speed rail network is the largest in the world. Some trains travel at over 300 km/h, making long trips between cities quick and comfortable.
